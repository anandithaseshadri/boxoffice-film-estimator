# boxoffice-film-estimator
This helps you decide whether a movie is worth investing in by estimating whether it could become a hit or a flop based on your choices.

### Investment. Hit. Flop.

The **Box Office Film Estimator** is an interactive tool that helps users explore a movie's potential box-office performance and investment success.

## Why Use It?

Ever wondered whether a movie is worth investing in?

This estimator allows users to explore how different film-related factors influence a movie's potential performance and whether it could become an investment success, a hit, or a flop.

## Features

- Interactive film investment estimator
- Scenario-based hit, flop and investment success verdicts
- Explore the influence of production budgets, actors, directors and genres
- Historical movie industry data from 1980–2020
- Interactive audience participation and decision-making

## Dataset

Uses historical movie industry data from 1980–2020 to explore trends in production budgets, box-office revenue, ratings and other industry factors.

## How to Use

1. Open the website.
2. Explore the interactive presentation.
3. Make your film investment choices.
4. View the estimated performance verdict.
5. Experiment with different choices and compare outcomes.

## Disclaimer

This is an educational and exploratory project. The estimator provides indicative, scenario-based verdicts and is not a statistically validated box-office prediction model or a substitute for actual investment advice.
